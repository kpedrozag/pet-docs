# Small pets

Rabbits, guinea pigs, and hamsters are good options for smaller homes, but each has specific needs.

| Pet        | Diet basics                         | Social?               |
|------------|-------------------------------------|-----------------------|
| Rabbit     | Unlimited hay, leafy greens, pellets | Yes — keep in pairs   |
| Guinea pig | Hay, vitamin C–rich vegetables       | Yes — keep in pairs   |
| Hamster    | Seed mix, small amounts of veggies   | No — keep alone       |

## Tips

- Rabbits and guinea pigs need hay at all times for teeth and digestion — see [Nutrition](./nutrition.md).
- Nails need trimming every 4–6 weeks — see [Grooming](./grooming.md).
- Rabbits can be litter-trained — see [Training](./training.md).
- Small pets hide illness; check [Health signs](./health-signs.md) often.
