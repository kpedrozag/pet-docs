# Vaccinations

Vaccines protect pets from serious diseases. Your vet will tailor the schedule; this is a general example.

## Dogs

| Age          | Vaccines                                   |
|--------------|--------------------------------------------|
| 6–8 weeks    | Distemper, parvovirus                      |
| 10–12 weeks  | DHPP (distemper, hepatitis, parainfluenza, parvo) |
| 12–16 weeks  | Rabies, DHPP booster                       |
| Yearly / every 3 years | Boosters as advised              |

## Cats

| Age          | Vaccines                                   |
|--------------|--------------------------------------------|
| 6–8 weeks    | FVRCP                                      |
| 10–12 weeks  | FVRCP booster, FeLV (if at risk)           |
| 12–16 weeks  | Rabies, FVRCP booster                      |

Mild tiredness after a vaccine is normal; swelling of the face or trouble breathing is an emergency — see [Health signs](./health-signs.md).
