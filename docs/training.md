# Training

Positive reinforcement — rewarding the behavior you want — is the most effective and humane training method.

## Basics

1. **Reward right away** (within 1–2 seconds) with a treat or praise.
2. **Keep sessions short:** 5–10 minutes, a few times a day.
3. **Be consistent:** everyone in the house uses the same words.
4. **Never punish** — it creates fear and makes training harder.

## First commands for dogs

`sit`, `down`, `stay`, `come`, and `leave it`. See [Dogs](./dogs.md).

## Other pets

- [Cats](./cats.md) can learn to use a scratching post and come when called.
- Rabbits can be litter-trained — see [Small pets](./small-pets.md).

Use small, healthy treats and count them in the daily [nutrition](./nutrition.md) budget.
