# Dogs

Dogs are social animals that need daily exercise, structure, and companionship.

## Exercise needs by size

| Size   | Example breeds              | Daily exercise |
|--------|-----------------------------|----------------|
| Small  | Chihuahua, Pug              | 30–60 min      |
| Medium | Beagle, Border Collie       | 1–2 hours      |
| Large  | Labrador, German Shepherd   | 1–2 hours      |

## Essentials

- Feed a complete diet — see [Nutrition](./nutrition.md).
- Keep [vaccinations](./vaccinations.md) up to date, including rabies.
- Start [training](./training.md) early with positive reinforcement.
- Brush regularly — see [Grooming](./grooming.md).

Watch for the warning signs in [Health signs](./health-signs.md).
