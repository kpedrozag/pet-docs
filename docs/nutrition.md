# Nutrition

Good nutrition is the foundation of pet health.

## General rules

- Feed a complete, balanced diet made for the species and life stage.
- Measure portions; obesity is the most common nutrition problem.
- Fresh water must always be available.
- Change foods gradually over 7–10 days.

## By pet

| Pet                         | Key point                                          |
|-----------------------------|----------------------------------------------------|
| [Dogs](./dogs.md)           | Two meals a day for adults; adjust for activity    |
| [Cats](./cats.md)           | Need animal protein and taurine; wet food helps hydration |
| [Small pets](./small-pets.md) | Hay should be most of the diet for rabbits and guinea pigs |
| [Fish](./fish.md)           | Small amounts once or twice a day                  |

## Toxic foods

Never feed chocolate, grapes, raisins, onions, garlic, xylitol, or alcohol. If your pet eats one, see [Health signs](./health-signs.md) and call your vet.
