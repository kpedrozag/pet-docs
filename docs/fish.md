# Fish

Fish are low-maintenance day to day, but their health depends on stable water.

## Tank setup

1. Choose the largest tank you can — bigger tanks stay stable more easily.
2. Add a filter and heater (for tropical fish).
3. **Cycle the tank** for 4–6 weeks before adding fish.
4. Add a few fish at a time.

## Water care

| Task                | How often   |
|---------------------|-------------|
| Test water          | Weekly      |
| Partial water change (20–25%) | Weekly |
| Rinse filter media in tank water | Monthly |

Feed only what they eat in two minutes — see [Nutrition](./nutrition.md). Cloudy eyes, clamped fins, or gasping at the surface are listed in [Health signs](./health-signs.md).
