# Health signs

Know what's normal for your pet so you notice changes early.

## Call the vet today if you see

- Not eating for more than 24 hours (12 hours for [small pets](./small-pets.md)).
- Vomiting or diarrhea lasting more than a day.
- Changes in litter box use or straining to pee — urgent for male [cats](./cats.md).
- Limping, lumps, or sudden weight change.

## Emergency — go now

- Trouble breathing or a swollen face (possible [vaccine](./vaccinations.md) reaction).
- Eating something toxic — see the list in [Nutrition](./nutrition.md).
- Collapse, seizures, or a swollen, hard belly in [dogs](./dogs.md).
- [Fish](./fish.md) gasping at the surface (test the water right away).

Regular [grooming](./grooming.md) is a good time to check for problems.
