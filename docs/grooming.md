# Grooming

Regular grooming keeps coats healthy and lets you spot problems early.

| Task          | Dogs                  | Cats                   | Small pets          |
|---------------|-----------------------|------------------------|---------------------|
| Brushing      | 1–7× per week by coat | 1–2× per week (daily for long hair) | Weekly; daily when shedding |
| Bathing       | Every 1–3 months      | Rarely                 | Avoid (spot clean)  |
| Nail trimming | Every 3–4 weeks       | Every 2–3 weeks        | Every 4–6 weeks     |
| Teeth         | Brush several times a week | Brush if tolerated | Hay wears teeth down |

## Tips

- Make grooming positive with treats — see [Training](./training.md).
- While brushing, check for lumps, ticks, and sore spots — see [Health signs](./health-signs.md).
