# Choosing a pet

Match the pet to your home, schedule, and budget.

| Pet                         | Daily time   | Space needed | Typical lifespan |
|-----------------------------|--------------|--------------|------------------|
| [Dog](./dogs.md)            | 1–3 hours    | Medium–large | 10–15 years      |
| [Cat](./cats.md)            | 30–60 min    | Small–medium | 12–18 years      |
| [Rabbit](./small-pets.md)   | 1–2 hours    | Medium       | 8–12 years       |
| [Hamster](./small-pets.md)  | 15–30 min    | Small        | 2–3 years        |
| [Fish](./fish.md)           | 10–15 min    | Small        | Varies           |

## Questions to ask yourself

1. Who will handle [feeding](./nutrition.md) and [grooming](./grooming.md) every day?
2. Can you afford [vaccinations](./vaccinations.md) and vet visits?
3. Do you have time for [training](./training.md)?
